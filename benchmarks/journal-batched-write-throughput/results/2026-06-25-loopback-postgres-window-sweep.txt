Notes
- At 1k-3k concurrent actors the batched journal is consistently ~1.4-2.2x faster than the default, even on loopback.
- Window length (3/5/10 ms) has only a minor effect on throughput at this concurrency: each round already fills batches
  regardless of window (max-requests=100 cap), so the window mostly affects latency, not peak throughput here. 5 ms was
  marginally best but within run-to-run noise.
- Batched throughput plateaus around 24k-26k ev/s. With actorCount >> max-requests this is bounded by
  batch.max-requests (100) and batch.parallelism (8); raising those is the next tuning lever (not varied here).
- The default journal numbers are window-independent; their spread (~12k-16k) reflects run-to-run variance.
